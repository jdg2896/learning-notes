# Writing Process

## Write, A Lot

- Prefer evergreen content (relevant in 5-10 years)
- What, How, Why, When, Who
- Write 1 million words
  - Write 1,000 words per day, 365 days a year
  - notes, blogs, journals
- Keep writing (jabbing)

## Mise en Place Writing

> The key insight of Mise en Place Writing is that we should decouple writing from pre-writing. With that, our writing improves, as well as our enjoyment of writing.

### Pre-writing

- Backlog grooming (ideas)
- Take notes as you go (second brain)

### Editing

- Settle for good enough
- Having a good editor would be nice
- Don't get bogged down trying to make a perfect blog post, it's okay to make mistakes

## Writing Prompts

- Introduction
  - What they'll learn
  - What your goals are
  - What they'll accomplish
- Content
  - What do they need to know?

## Note-Taking

If you're using Apple devices (MacBook, iPhone, etc.), Apple Notes is good enough for note-taking. You don't need to search for other note-taking apps, especially if you haven't found one yet.

- On Mac, quickly add notes by hovering over the bottom-right hot corner.
- On iPhone, set up a shortcut to quickly open a new note.
