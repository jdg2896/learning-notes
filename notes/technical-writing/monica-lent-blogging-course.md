# Monica Lent's Blogging Course

## Day 1

- **Publish** on your **own domain name**
- **Syndicate** content the right way
  - canonical URLs

## Day 2

- **Maintain** an idea log
- **Determine** the best medium
- **Validate** your idea with research
  - SEO

## Day 3

- SEO means **free, recurring traffic!**
- Discover **keywords**
- Niche down to a **main keyword**
- Find related **tail keywords**
- Include keywords in strategic places
- Craft an **excellent title**
- **Tools**
  - https://keywordseverywhere.com/

## Day 4

Before writing, have:

- A **main keyword** you want to write about
- A list of **tail keywords and topics** to include for a complete post
- A list of **external sites** you can link to
- An **outline of your post** with headlines and subheadlines
- A couple of **ideas for your headline**

Tips:

- Headline is king
- Make your post easier to scan with varied formatting
- Shorten the intro (and the whole post)
- Internal cliff-hangers (use sparingly)
- Make it relatable
- Use humor
- Be specific
- End with a call-to-action

## Day 5

- Create content that demonstrates an intimate understanding of your audience.
- Email lists are extremely powerful, and I should start one
  - ConvertKit

## Day 6

Before publishing:

- Canonical tag
- Google Analytics
- Google Search Console
- Social sharing metadata
- Email list
- Spellcheck and grammar check
- Revisit your title again
- Ask a friend for feedback
- Sleep on content before publishing

## Day 7

After publishing:

- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console
- Share content on Twitter
- Post article in Facebook groups
- Post article on LinkedIn
- Post article on Facebook page
- Create a Pinterest pin
- Post article on Reddit
- Hacker News
- Syndicate article
- Send new article to email list
