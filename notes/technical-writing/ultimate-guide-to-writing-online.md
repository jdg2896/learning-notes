# The Ultimate Guide to Writing Online (David Perell)

[Article link](https://perell.com/essay/the-ultimate-guide-to-writing-online/)

## Write from Abundance

### 1. Upgrade Your Information Diet

- Find better ingredients
- Escape the never-ending now
- Curate information streams
  - short-form: **follow smart people on social media**
  - medium-form: **read timeless ideas**
  - long-form: **read more books, watch old documentaries, listen to lectures**

### 2. Practice the Capture Habit

- Ambient research
- Read-it-later apps: **Instapaper**
- Capture your own ideas
- Notice where you already write

### 3. Build a Note-Taking System

- collect the dots
- connect the dots

## Write from Conversation

Writing doesn't need to be so lonely.

### 4. Conversation with Friends

- Speaking to generate ideas (Otter app)
- Writing to refine ideas
- Look for surprises (test your ideas)
- Avoid writer brain

### 5. Conversation with Readers

- Test ideas at scale
  - Twitter and email
  - Ask your audience questions
- Feedback-driven book
  - Cultivate feedback into your process
- Newsletter replies

### 6. Conversation with Editors

- Honest, critical advice
  - Small network of trusted people
- Types of feedback
- Ask editors for CRIBS:
  - **C**onfusing
  - **R**epeated
  - **I**nteresting
  - **B**oring
  - **S**urprising
- Expression is compression

## Write in Public

- Don't fear nothingness
- Selling is part of life, do your own marketing
- Audience building takes time
- Find people on public platforms (Twitter, etc.), build relationships with them on **private ones** (email, personal blog, etc.)

### 7. Engage the Public

- Find your public platform
  - Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn
  - small forums, Discord servers, Slack, etc.
  - Focus on one platform
- Add value to platforms
  - fit the platform (Twitter for threads, Medium for long-form, etc.)
- Direct people to your digital real estate
  - link to personal website, newsletter, etc.

### 8. Build Your Online Home

- Personal website
- Navigation
  - start here
  - photos of yourself
  - short introduction
  - quick bio
  - list of interests
  - where you've worked
  - what you're working on
  - featured essays
  - book recommendations
  - links to podcasts you've been on
  - your life story in 50 words or less
- Essay categories
  - organize your articles
  - don't sort by chronological order
  - categorize by:
    - topic
    - length
    - format
    - date
    - tags
    - recommended articles
- Aesthetics
  - a unique look that defines who you are
- Getting started
  - start simple, but distinct
  - capture emails
    - start here page
    - footer
    - essays/articles

### 9. Start an Email Newsletter

- Recurring attention
  - email subscribers are digital gold
- Email is sticky
